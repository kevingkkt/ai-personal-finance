# Income and Expense Tracker Guide

## Income

Income is money received by the user. Examples include salary, gifts, freelance work and other payments.

Recording income helps users understand how much money is available before expenses are considered.

## Expenses

Expenses are money spent by the user. Common expense categories include food, transport, bills, shopping, entertainment and groceries.

Recording expenses helps users identify where their money is being spent.

## Remaining Money

Remaining money is calculated by subtracting total expenses from total income.

Remaining Money = Total Income - Total Expenses

If expenses are greater than income, the user is spending more money than they receive.

## Saving

A user may choose to save part of their remaining money.

The Income and Expense Tracker currently uses a suggested saving percentage of 60 percent of the remaining money.

Suggested Saving = Remaining Money × 60%

The suggested saving amount should not be greater than the remaining money.

## Spending Categories

Grouping expenses into categories helps users understand which areas have the highest spending.

The highest spending category is the category with the largest total expense amount.

## Transaction Records

Each transaction contains:

- Type: income or expense
- Category
- Amount
- Date
- Description

Keeping transaction records up to date improves the accuracy of spending summaries and saving calculations.

## Limitations

The Income and Expense Tracker provides simple educational financial information.

It does not provide professional investment, tax, credit or financial planning advice.
