# Budget Manager Guide

## Budgets
A budget is a planned amount assigned a name and a date range. Examples include a grocery budget, an entertainment budget or a utilities budget.

A budget entry contains:
- Name: The budget label or category.
- Amount: The planned amount for that budget.
- Start Date: The first date covered by the budget.
- End Date: The last date covered by the budget.
- Description: Optional context about the budget.

## Creating and Managing Budget
Users can add, edit and delete budget entries. Each entry can be reviewed in the budgets list alongside its amount, date range and description.

## Budget Summaries
The Budget Manager summarizes the entries provided. Its summary can include the total and average budget amounts, the largest and smallest entries, the top categories by amount, and each entry's share of the total.

The summary can also identify date ranges that overlap, entries ending within 30 days or already past their end date, and possible trim candidates. It groups entries by keywords in their names and descriptions, such as food, transport, housing, health and lifestyle.

## AI Budget Insights
AI Budget Insights uses the current budget entries to describe the largest category, suggest a possible trim target and comment on balance between categories. Suggestions are based on the budget plan and its category amounts.

## Limitations
The Budget Manager stores planned budget amounts; it does not record actual purchases or calculate how much has been spent or remains. Its summaries and insights cannot determine whether actual spending is within budget.

AI insights are informational suggestions based on the available budget entries. They are not professional financial, investment, tax, loan or credit advice.
